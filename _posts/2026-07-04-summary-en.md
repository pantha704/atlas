## [South Korea Plans to Send Civilian Lunar Module to the Moon in 2030](https://cn.yna.co.kr/view/ACK20260703002200881) ⭐️ 5.0/10

South Korea plans to send a civilian lunar module to the moon in 2030 using its domestically developed 'World' rocket. This plan is significant as it marks South Korea's ambitious goal to explore space and demonstrate its capabilities in rocket technology. The 'World' rocket is a domestically developed launch vehicle, and the lunar module will be sent to the moon in 2030, with a follow-up mission planned for 2032.

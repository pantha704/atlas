The HDMI 2.2 specification has been released, doubling the bandwidth to 96 Gbps and enabling uncompressed 4K and 8K video transmission. This is significant because it enables higher resolutions and faster refresh rates for video transmission, which will benefit users with high-end displays and graphics cards. The new specification includes multiple bandwidth levels, including 64 Gbps, 80 Gbps, and 96 Gbps, and introduces the LIP protocol to improve audio-video synchronization.
